Pasta alla Carbonara

Pasta alla carbonara is an iconic italian dish that stands out due to its simplicity and technique. The original recipe doesn't have cream, milk, butter or garlic; its creaminess comes exclusively from the emulsion of egg yolks with the cheese and the water used to cook the pasta.

Ingredients (for two):

  • 200g of spaghetti
  • 100g-150g of guanciale
  • 60g of finely grated Pecorino Romano
  • 3 to 4 egg yolks
  • Pepper
  • Salt

Steps:

  1. Preparing the Guanciale: Cut the guanciale into strips or cubes. Fry in a pan over medium heat until the fat melts and the meat is golden brown and crispy. Reserve the melted fat in the pan.
  2. Preparing the cream: In a bowl, mix the egg yolks with most of the Pecorino Romano and a generous amount of black pepper. Whisk until you obtain a thick paste.
  3. Cooking the Pasta: Cook the pasta in boiling water with a little salt until al dente (remove 1–2 minutes before the time indicated on the package). Important: Reserve a cup of the cooking water before draining.
  4. Emulsifying: Add the drained pasta to the pan with the guanciale and its fat. Sauté for one minute.
  5. The Finishing Touch: Remove the pan from the heat (this step is crucial to avoid "boiling" the eggs and turning them into scrambled eggs). Pour the egg and cheese mixture over the pasta. Add some of the reserved cooking water and stir vigorously to create a silky, creamy sauce.
  6. Serve immediately with the remaining cheese and more pepper on top.
